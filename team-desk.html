Start your shift on The Service Floor

We are doctors in services, so think of this floor the way a clinic thinks of its day: calls come in, stations take them, and the board shows what is next. Find your station, enter your passcode, and work your call board. No new account, no Google sign-in, no Ziggy sign-in, and no extra cost.

Why the old desk-plus-two-digits key is retired. A station code is public, there are only 50 stations, and a two-digit number has only 100 options. That is 50 times 100, or 5,000 guesses in total, and anyone could try them one desk at a time. That is not true security. The old example P03387 is retired and must not be used as a passcode.
What a real passcode is here. Your station code is your public station name, like a username. It is not secret. Your passcode is secret, separate, and at least 8 characters long, with letters and numbers. H01 sets it when you are seated, and it is never written into this page, into a repo, or into Drive. Your passcode stays in your pocket: this page does not store it. It sends it only to the Worker check on this site, at /api/station-login, when that Worker is live, and to nowhere else.
True security note, read this before real work goes on the board. The Worker check is built in src/index.js: the page sends station plus passcode to /api/station-login, the Worker checks a salted hash kept as a secret, never the passcode itself, locks a station for 15 minutes after 5 failed tries, and returns an 8-hour session. If that Worker is not live yet, the page falls back to a sample board only, with a warning, and does not claim verification. Real jobs must not go on the board until that Worker check is live and tested.
